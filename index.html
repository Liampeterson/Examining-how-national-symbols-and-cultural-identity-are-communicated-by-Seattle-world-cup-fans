Supporters' Archive · Field Study, Seattle WA

Symbols & ritual in fan identity

Field Log

This archive presents four photo essays documenting how Seattle-based World Cup fans use flags, jerseys, chants, and costumes to express identity and belonging. Field notes, interviews, and photographs were collected during a summer humanities research program based in Seattle, Washington, with fieldwork conducted at watch parties, fan marches, and match-day gatherings around the city.
