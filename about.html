Project Overview

After much preparation and anticipation, the layered delights and dooms; the 2026 FIFA Men's World Cup has arrived to Seattle. For the first time since 2002 when Japan and South Korea co-hosted the event, we have a transnational tournament with sixteen host cities holding matches in Canada, the United States and Mexico. For locals visiting teams and fans on the continent, their experiences will be especially unique to their locations. Six games will take place in the stadium the Seattle Reign, Seattle Sounders and Seattle Seahawks call home and the Husky Soccer Stadium at UW will also welcome their pitch to visiting teams. Around the stadium and throughout the region for the breadth of the cup, there are watch parties, themed activities, menu specials, civil disobedience, extra bus routes and more which are leveraged on this global event touching Seatown's people, soil and cement.
