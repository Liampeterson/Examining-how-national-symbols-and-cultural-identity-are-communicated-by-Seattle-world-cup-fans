About

SIAH

SIAH (Summer Institute in the Arts & Humanities) is a summer research program that pairs students with a humanities field methodology — participant observation, interviews, and photography — and asks them to turn it into a public-facing project. This archive is the output of one team's 2026 summer: a season of fieldwork among Seattle-based World Cup supporters, documenting how flags, jerseys, chants, and costumes are used to perform identity and belonging.
